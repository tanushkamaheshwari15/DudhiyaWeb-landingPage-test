Refund Policy

Introduction

At Dudhiya, we strive to ensure that your experience with our app is smooth and satisfying. However, in certain situations, you may be eligible for a refund. This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds may be issued.

Non-Refundable Transactions

  • Once you add money to your wallet, it is non-refundable after 3 days.
  • If an entry is made incorrectly and later deleted, the money will not be refunded.
  • Payments for services and transactions processed via the wallet are not refundable beyond the aforementioned periods.

Refund Process

If you have a concern regarding a transaction or if money was deducted from your bank account but not reflected in the wallet, you can raise a ticket.

  • The request for refunds or wallet adjustments should be raised within 3 days from the issue.
  • Refunds for valid claims will be processed and credited back to the wallet or bank account within 7-15 working days from when the ticket is raised.

How to Request a Refund

If you believe you are eligible for a refund or need assistance with your wallet or transaction, please raise a ticket in the "Ticket Section" of the app.

For further inquiries, contact us at info@netpy.com.

Changes to Refund Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this refund policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected here, and the updated date will be noted at the bottom.
