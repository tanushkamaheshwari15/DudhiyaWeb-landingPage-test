Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans anytime?

expand_more

Yes! You can upgrade from Free Trial to Monthly, Yearly or Wallet Recharge at any time directly through your dashboard settings.

How does the Wallet Recharge plan charge me?

expand_more

In the Wallet plan, there are no fixed monthly costs. You only pay ₹0.0125 per kg of milk collected. For example, if you collect 100 kg of milk, only ₹1.25 is deducted from your wallet balance. You can recharge with as low as ₹10 to keep using the services.

Does my wallet balance expire?

expand_more

No! Your wallet balance has lifetime validity and will never expire, even if your collection center remains closed during seasonal off-times.
