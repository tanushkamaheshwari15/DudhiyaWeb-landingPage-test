What Does the Buyer (Collection Center) Do?

It's important to understand that Suppliers (Farmers) and Buyers (Collection Centers) have different roles in Dudhiya. Here's what the Buyer does:

admin_panel_settings Admin Role
edit_note Records Data
admin_panel_settings Admin Role
edit_note Records Data

Buyer Responsibilities

1

Add & Manage Suppliers

The Buyer (Collection Center) adds farmers to their system and manages supplier profiles with complete details.

2

Record All Collections

Only Buyers can add/edit milk collections. When you deliver milk, they record quantity, FAT, SNF, and calculate payments.

3

Generate & Share Reports

Buyers create PDF/Excel reports and bills, then share them with you via WhatsApp or email. You can only VIEW these reports.

info Remember: As a Supplier, you can only VIEW data. The Buyer manages all the data entry and report generation.
