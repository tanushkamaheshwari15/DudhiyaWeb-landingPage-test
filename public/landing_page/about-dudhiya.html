apps What is Dudhiya

The Smart Dairy Management Ecosystem

Dudhiya is a comprehensive digital platform designed to transform how milk collection centers and dairy farmers manage their operations. It replaces traditional paper registers, manual calculations, and complicated spreadsheets with an intuitive, automated system.

Built specifically for the Indian dairy industry, Dudhiya understands the unique challenges of milk collection, FAT/SNF calculations, farmer payments, and report generation. Our platform handles everything from recording daily collections to generating automated payment reports.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dudhiya free to use? expand_more
Yes! For farmers (suppliers), Dudhiya is completely free. For collection centers (buyers), the first month is free. After that, affordable subscription plans are available based on your collection volume.
Can I switch between Buyer and Supplier roles? expand_more
Yes! You can easily switch roles from the app settings. Each role has its own dashboard and features tailored to that user type.
Is my data secure? expand_more
Absolutely. All data is encrypted and stored securely in the cloud with automatic backups. Even if you lose your phone, your data remains safe.
