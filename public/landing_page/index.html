Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Dudhiya

How much does Dudhiya cost? expand_more

For Suppliers (Farmers): Completely FREE. Suppliers can track their milk deliveries, view earnings, and manage their profile at no cost.

For Buyers (Collection Centers): FREE for the first month. After the trial, subscription plans are available based on your collection volume and wallet features.

Download the app and start your free trial today — no credit card required!

Is my data safe and backed up? expand_more

Yes, your data is securely stored in the cloud with automatic backups. Even if you lose your phone or switch devices, your data remains safe and can be accessed instantly when you log in.

How do I switch between Buyer and Supplier roles? expand_more

You can switch roles easily from the app settings. Tap on your profile, select "Switch Role," and choose between Buyer (Collection Center) or Supplier (Farmer) mode. Each role has its own dashboard and features.

What kind of reports can I generate? expand_more

Dudhiya lets you generate comprehensive reports including: Collection Reports (daily, weekly, monthly), Farmer Wallet Statements, Payment Summaries, and Shift-wise Reports. All reports can be exported as PDF or Excel and shared instantly via WhatsApp.
