Privacy Policy

Introduction

At Dudhiya, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal information when you use our services. By using our app, you agree to the collection and use of your data in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Details: Name, address, phone number, email address, and any other information you provide when signing up or interacting with the app.
  • Milk Collection Details: Data related to the daily milk collection, including milk rate, milk quantity, SNF, Fat percentage, etc.
  • Wallet Information: Transaction history and amount in the wallet for processing payments.
  • User Interaction Data: Information related to how you interact with the app.

Use of Information

The data we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • To provide you with the services and functionality of the Dudhiya app.
  • To process payments and transactions in the wallet.
  • To generate weekly reports for the milk collector.
  • To resolve any issues or disputes related to transactions and tickets.
  • To communicate with you about updates, features, and promotions related to the app.
